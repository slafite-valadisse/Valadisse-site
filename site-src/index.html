FAQ

Common questions

What is ads.txt?

ads.txt is a public file on a publisher's site that lists every company authorized to sell its ad inventory. Buyers check it before they bid, and supply that is not declared there is often filtered out before the auction starts.

What is sellers.json?

sellers.json is the matching file an exchange publishes, listing the sellers it represents. Buyers compare it with a publisher's ads.txt to confirm that the path from them to the publisher is real.

Why do some bids never arrive?

Buyers filter supply before an auction starts. A missing exchange, a long reseller chain, or duplicated or unverifiable entries in ads.txt can keep a buyer from bidding at all. Those skipped bids never reach the ad server, so standard reports do not show them.

What does the free scan show, and what does Starter add?

The free scan reads a site's ads.txt and returns a Supply Chain Health Score, a floor signal and how many premium exchanges are missing, with no signup. Starter names the missing exchanges, compares each rescan with the last, sends a weekly report by email, and calculates the Floor Optimization Index and Demand Diversity scores from an uploaded ad server report.

Does Valadisse take a revenue share or have platform ties?

No. Starter and Pro are a flat monthly price, and Advisory is priced by engagement. Valadisse takes no revenue share and has no affiliation with any exchange, platform or supply partner.
