Questions

Before you scan.

What is Valadisse?

Valadisse is a diagnostic service for independent digital publishers. It shows why ad inventory is being filtered out of buyer demand and what to fix.

Who is Valadisse for?

Independent digital publishers that sell advertising programmatically.

What does the free scan show?

A Supply Chain Health Score, a floor signal and how many exchanges are missing from a publisher’s ad supply chain. Paid plans add the named exchanges, scheduled monitoring, change alerts and a weekly report.

What is ads.txt?

A public file listing every company allowed to sell a site’s ad inventory. Buyers check it before they bid, and supply missing from it is often filtered out.

What is sellers.json?

The matching file an exchange publishes, listing the sellers it represents. Buyers compare it with ads.txt to confirm the path to a publisher is real.

Does Valadisse take a revenue share?

No. Valadisse has no revenue share and no platform ties.
